learning systems for teams that want more capability without losing control.

Our work combines product engineering, AI agents, custom software, and
evidence-led growth. Humans retain direction, taste, approval, and
accountability. Software and agents earn trust through clear authority,
observable results, and honest failure.

## How we publish

Every public repository needs a named owner, an explicit license, a current
status, a security-reporting route, and claims bounded by evidence. Private
prototypes, customer work, and protected partner systems stay private.
